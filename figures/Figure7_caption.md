**Figure 7. Blocking the alternative oxidase potentiates prothioconazole at 27 °C but not at 15 °C.** Prothioconazole (0, 2 and 4 mg L⁻¹) and SHAM (0 or 0.2 mM) in a full factorial on one plate per temperature, three independent cultures each run as a paired 15 °C and 27 °C plate from the same inoculated cups (n = 3 per temperature; 5 wells for vehicle and SHAM alone, 3 wells per drug condition). Growth rates were estimated from dissolved-oxygen traces with the same oxygen-consumption model and fitting procedure used throughout (all traces, intervals and fits in Fig. S18). (a, b) Growth rate relative to the vehicle control on the same plate at 15 °C (a) and 27 °C (b): small symbols, plate means (symbol shape identifies the culture); large symbols and bars, mean ± s.d. across cultures. For each combination the open diamond is the growth rate expected if the drug and SHAM acted independently, the product of their separate relative effects on the same plate, and the arrow is the departure from it. At 15 °C SHAM alone has no effect and the combinations fall where independence predicts; at 27 °C the combinations fall well below it. (c) The interaction contrast I = log r_PS − log r_P − log r_S + log r_V per culture at each temperature (zero, independence; negative, SHAM potentiates the drug), paired within culture (grey lines), with the mean and 95% confidence interval. The within-culture temperature difference I₂₇ − I₁₅ at 2 mg L⁻¹ (the prespecified primary contrast) is -0.61 (95% CI -1.81 to +0.59; paired t, P = 0.16), negative in all three cultures; at 4 mg L⁻¹ it is -0.44 (P = 0.23). Because the contrast spans all four rows of the plate with weights +1, −1, −1, +1, any linear positional gradient cancels in I. (d) The fitted oxygen-consumption parameter K against the growth rate r, both relative to vehicle, for every plate × condition: the drug lowers r while K is maintained or raised, and the combinations at 27 °C are the extreme of that pattern, i.e. the cells respire but do not multiply, the signature of inhibition rather than death. Fitting intervals were rule based (post-equilibration maximum to the point where oxygen fell below 2.5 mg L⁻¹, and no later than 40.5 h on the 27 °C plates so that all conditions were fitted over the same stretch of the run). Before fitting, the offset caused by briefly removing the plates at 41 h for an interim export was subtracted and a 4 h centred moving average was applied to remove the re-aeration cycle on the 27 °C plates; fits on the unsmoothed traces are given alongside in `tables/aox/ptc_sham_well_rates.csv` (Methods).
