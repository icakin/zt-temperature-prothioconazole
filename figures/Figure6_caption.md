**Figure 6. SHAM reduces growth of *Zymoseptoria tritici* at 27 °C but not at 15 °C,
and abolishes azide-resistant respiration.**

**(a)** Exponential growth rate *r* against SHAM concentration at 15 °C (blue circles) and
27 °C (red triangles). Rates were obtained by fitting O(t) = O₂,₀ + (K/r)(1 − e^{rt}) to
sealed-vial dissolved-oxygen traces (all traces and fits in Fig. S16). The narrow strip at
the left shows solvent controls; dashed horizontal lines carry the control means across the
dose panel. Small pale points are the three independent cultures at each dose, faint lines
their individual regressions on log₁₀[SHAM], bold lines the mean of the three culture
slopes. Filled symbols with bars are the mean ± s.d. across cultures. A 0.35 mM treatment
was also run and omitted because at 27 °C the rate was not identifiable for two of three
cultures (Methods; Fig. S12).

**(b)** Per-culture regression slopes of *r* on log₁₀[SHAM], with the mean and its 95%
confidence interval. Culture is the unit of replication (n = 3 per temperature). The
difference in mean slope is −0.0238 h⁻¹ per tenfold (Welch's unequal-variance *t*-test,
P = 0.020); an exact permutation of temperature labels across the six slopes places the
observed contrast as the most extreme of the 20 possible assignments (one-sided P = 0.050,
the design floor).

**(c)** Dissolved oxygen at 27 °C in sealed SensorDish wells containing no cells (grey),
0.5 mM sodium azide to block cytochrome oxidase (orange), or azide plus 1 mM SHAM (purple).
Lines are condition means for each of three independent plates (biological replicates;
n = 10, 7 and 7 wells per plate). Shading marks the interval used for rate estimation, from
the post-equilibration maximum to +10 h; every well and its fit are shown in Fig. S17.

**(d)** Initial O₂ consumption rate per well from a linear fit over that interval
(mean R² = 0.96). Small markers, wells; large markers, plate means, joined within plate;
grey band, cell-free wells (mean ± s.d. of plate means). Azide-resistant respiration was
reduced to cell-free background by SHAM in every plate (paired *t*-test on plate means,
n = 3, P = 0.012). Because 0.2 mM SHAM only partially suppressed growth at 27 °C (a),
respirometry used 1 mM, a concentration commonly used for alternative-oxidase inhibition in
fungi; the result is consistent with alternative-oxidase-dependent respiratory capacity at
27 °C, although the assay alone does not exclude reversible off-target effects at this
concentration (Discussion).
