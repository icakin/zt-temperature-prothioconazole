# Preregistration — AOX inhibitor experiment (FROZEN BEFORE DATA)

**Frozen: 2026-08-20, before any inhibitor experiment has been run. The model
predictions below were generated by `scripts/86_aox_counterfactual.py` (outputs
`tables/revision/v4/aox_counterfactual.csv`, `86_log.txt`) with growth RELEASED
as an output; nothing here was tuned to any inhibitor data, which do not yet
exist. This document is the timestamped hypothesis; the experiment is the test.**

## The question
The manuscript establishes AOX induction as a MARKER of the low-CUE stress
state under prothioconazole and heat, and explicitly does not establish it as a
causal MECHANISM. This experiment discriminates the two.

## Frozen model predictions
- **P1 (growth, inhibitor alone):** blocking AOX changes maximum feasible
  growth by < 5% in every condition — in fact by ≈ 0% in all runs: plain ztGEM
  (default, ×0.5, ×2 maintenance; enriched medium; 200 uncertainty draws per
  condition) and ecZtGEM all give Δ = 0.000. The network compensates through
  the cytochrome chain at negligible cost.
- **P2 (growth, combination):** AOX inhibition adds NO growth reduction beyond
  prothioconazole alone, at any temperature (no drug × inhibitor synergy).
- **P3 (respiration, bracketing observable):** the condition-constrained
  analysis attributes 13% (15 °C), 24% (21 °C) and 42% (27 °C) of oxygen
  consumption to AOX in the drug conditions. If AOX truly carries that flux in
  vivo and acute compensation is limited, blocking it should REDUCE measured
  respiration by up to those shares (largest at 27 °C + drug); full network
  compensation predicts little change. Respiration under inhibition therefore
  brackets the two readings; growth (P1/P2) is the decisive outcome.

## Outcome map (decided now)
- Growth unchanged by inhibitor, alone and with drug, at all temperatures →
  marker interpretation CONFIRMED; the GEM counterfactual is VALIDATED (a
  genuine prospective validation of the reconstruction).
- Reproducible growth defect under inhibitor + prothioconazole beyond drug
  alone (relative reduction > 10% with 95% CI excluding 5%), largest at 27 °C →
  model compensation FALSIFIED; AOX elevated from marker to causal contributor;
  Discussion updated accordingly. This outcome is scientifically the more
  exciting one — the prereg protects it from being called post hoc.
- Inhibitor alone reduces growth at concentrations below validated AOX
  blockade → off-target toxicity; the discriminating contrast is uninterpretable
  at that concentration; use the pilot to avoid this regime.

## Design recommendations (advisory, not gates)
1. **Inhibitor & dose pilot:** SHAM; typical fungal range ~0.1–5 mM. Establish
   the working concentration with the POSITIVE CONTROL: SHAM should strongly
   synergise with a QoI (e.g. azoxystrobin) in this species — the established
   AOX-rescue effect — proving AOX blockade at that dose. SHAM has documented
   off-target activity in fungi; use the lowest validated concentration.
2. **Off-target control:** SHAM-alone dose–response on growth at a benign
   temperature (21/24 °C); the chosen dose must not depress control growth.
3. **Core factorial:** 15/21(or 24)/27 °C × prothioconazole (0, 2 mg L⁻¹) ×
   SHAM (0, working dose), ≥3 vials, same PreSens DO pipeline and oxygen-model
   fitting as the manuscript, so growth and respiration are directly comparable.
   If capacity is limited, priority conditions: 27 °C block (largest predicted
   AOX share, antagonism region) and 15 °C block (synergy region).
4. **Analysis (predefined):** interaction contrast on log growth
   (drug+SHAM vs drug) per temperature with the existing Bayesian machinery;
   respiration change under SHAM per condition. No new metrics invented after
   seeing data.

## Manuscript handling
The restrained counterfactual prediction and its falsification criterion are
inserted in the Discussion NOW (timestamped by this repo), so the experiment
tests a preregistered, written prediction. If results arrive before submission,
they enter the manuscript as a validation/falsification of that prediction —
either outcome strengthens the paper.
